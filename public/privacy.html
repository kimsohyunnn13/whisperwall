Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Welcome to Whisper Wall. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we receive and collect when you use our website, as well as how we safeguard your data.

Anonymity Commitment: We do not require user account registration, real names, phone numbers, or email addresses to post notes on this board. Your messages are completely anonymous and automatically deleted permanently within 12 to 24 hours.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to the absolute bare minimum:

  • Anonymous Note Content: The text content and category you submit to the public board. These notes are ephemeral and are purged from our database automatically upon expiration.
  • Technical & Anti-Abuse Logs: To prevent spamming and automated bot abuse, our server temporarily processes a one-way hash of client IP addresses in-memory solely for rate-limiting purposes. This information is never permanently stored nor linked to any personal identity.
  • Local Storage (Browser Storage): We store your UI preferences (e.g., Silent Room dark mode, board display language) locally in your browser so your experience remains seamless across sessions.

2. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

Our website may use third-party cookies for performance and advertising delivery:

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. User Generated Content & Community Safety

Users are strictly prohibited from submitting content that promotes hate speech, harassment, defamation, pornography, threats of violence, personal identifiable information (doxxing), or illegal activities. A community reporting tool (*Report*) is available on every note to flag violations for immediate moderation.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page along with an updated revision date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request an urgent note takedown, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
